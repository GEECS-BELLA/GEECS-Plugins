Trajectory Composer

Describe the sweep — how many axes, how they are spaced, whether they move together or cross — and the stock bluesky.plans verb falls out of it. Nothing here talks to a worker: it resolves the plan, validates it the way the plan layer would, and draws the points it would visit.

      why these are separate
      
          
      
            
      These three carry fully typed signatures — named parameters,
              not the *args the classic family uses. So they need no axis-row form at all: their
              parameters are the form, and a generic signature-driven renderer would handle them
              correctly. They are the evidence that the composer on the other tab is a workaround for
              varargs, not a universal answer.
