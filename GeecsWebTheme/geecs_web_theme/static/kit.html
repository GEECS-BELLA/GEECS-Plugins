The shell

This page is the specimen: sticky topbar, 216 px rail, one pane. Below 900 px the rail becomes a strip and nothing else moves — narrow the window. Both controls in the topbar are the kit's own, and both persist across every GEECS surface on this origin.

The five states every pane owes

Loading and empty exist on both surfaces today in private forms. Error, stale and denied exist nowhere — and a control surface cannot open without the last two. Error and stale take a colour; denied takes a dashed edge — not an alarm, a door someone else is holding.
