    {# How shot filters work (rung 0: native details, no script). #}
    

      How shot filters work (with examples)
      

        
Each line reads as a sentence: keep shots where [column] is between (or not between)
          [low] and [high]. Pick the column from its menu (click it and start typing to jump);
          type plain numbers in the two boxes — never <, > or =.
          Leave a box empty for no limit on that side. Both ends are included.

        
Examples

        
    
          
  • Charge between 10 and 50: U_ICT charge · between · 10 and 50.
    • 
          
  • Charge above 10 (no upper limit): U_ICT charge · between · 10 and (empty).
    • 
          
  • Jet position below 3: jet position · between · (empty) and 3.
    • 
          
  • Leave out a bad window, 2 to 3: jet position · not between · 2 and 3.
    • 
        

        
A group keeps a shot only if all its lines hold (AND): charge 10 to 50 and jet
          below 3 is one group with two lines (+ AND condition). With several groups a shot is kept if any
          group keeps it (OR): add + OR group for a second, alternative window.

        
The count under the groups says how many shots would pass before you Apply. In the rail, a
          group's tick turns it off without deleting it; ✕ deletes it. "Rows with NaN" says whether a missing
          value passes or fails a line.

        
Both pages keep the same shots: the Plot page's plots and 2-D map, and the Images strips (a bin
          averages only its passing shots; a bin with none drops out).
